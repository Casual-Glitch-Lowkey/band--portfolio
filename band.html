Radiohead

Official website: https://www.radiohead.com/

Basic Band Facts

Formed: 1985

Origin: Abingdon, Oxfordshire, England

Genres: Alternative rock, art rock, experimental rock, electronic

Record labels: XL Recordings, Capitol, Parlophone (earlier years)

Status: Active

Band Members

  • Thom Yorke – lead vocals, guitar, piano
  • Jonny Greenwood – lead guitar, keyboards, orchestration
  • Ed O’Brien – guitar, backing vocals
  • Colin Greenwood – bass guitar
  • Philip Selway – drums, percussion

Favorite Album Releases (with Reviews)

OK Computer (1997)
A dramatic, layered album that captures the anxiety of modern life. It mixes memorable melodies with inventive production and big emotional moments.
Kid A (2000)
A bold shift into electronic and experimental sounds. It feels mysterious and immersive, and it rewards repeated listening because there’s always another detail to notice.
In Rainbows (2007)
Warm and human, with a smooth flow from track to track. It balances beautiful vocals, strong rhythms, and emotional songwriting without feeling overproduced.
